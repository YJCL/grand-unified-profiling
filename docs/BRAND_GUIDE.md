# Orba Brand Guide v1.0

The machine-readable visual source of truth is [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md). The public expression is available at `/brand`.

## Brand core

- Purpose: 自分を知り、選び直せる言葉を増やす。
- Promise: 複数の知恵を、今日使える一つの実感へ。
- Personality: Precise · Warm · Still.
- Brand line: 精密に確かめ、温かく言葉にする。

## Identity status

| Element | Status | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Orba` wordmark in Cormorant Garamond Medium Italic | Confirmed | Primary identity in every surface. |
| Luminous gold orb mark | Provisional v1 | Favicon, compact lockup, app mark. |
| WebGL sphere and orbit ellipses | Expression system | Hero, brand storytelling, focus moments. Not the logo. |
| Old incomplete ring mark | Retired | Do not use. |

## Product expression

- LP: **Celestial Instrument** — a dimensional focal orb, editorial thesis, long quiet sections.
- Service: **Observation Paper** — a dark application shell holding readable paper surfaces.
- Social / motion: crop the orb and orbit system into one focal composition; do not fill layouts with generic stars.

## Language

Use calm, specific language that leaves agency with the user. Prefer “ひとつの見方です” and “気になる場所だけを深く読めます.” Avoid certainty, fear, inflated intelligence claims, and mystical jargon without a user benefit.
