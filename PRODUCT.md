# Orba Product Context

## Product

Orba is a personal reflection partner that combines eight layers of knowledge with the user's own words. It does not hand down a single deterministic answer. It helps a person see a clearer outline of themselves and make choices with more agency.

## Core users

- People who are carrying a decision, transition, or unresolved feeling.
- People who like astrology, profiling, and reflective tools but do not want a disposable fortune result.
- Returning members who use dialogue, calendar rhythm, and their evolving profile together.

## Core product loop

1. Meet and choose a partner orb.
2. Share enough context for an initial profile.
3. Read a concise synthesis before opening deeper layers.
4. Return through daily dialogue and the calendar.
5. Refine the profile through lived language over time.

## Durable constraints

- Existing API routes, Prisma data, authentication, billing, chat, push, and calculation behavior are production truth.
- Marketing is `/`, first-time profiling is `/start`, and returning users live primarily in `/mypage`, `/chat`, and `/calendar`.
- Brand promise: precise, warm, and still.
- Public language must avoid deterministic or fear-based claims.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; the WebGL orb must retain a static fallback.
- The luminous orb symbol is provisional v1. The wordmark `Orba` is primary.
